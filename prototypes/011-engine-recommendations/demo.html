Engine prototype — does tag-overlap recommend well?

Wayfinder ticket 011 · throwaway, answers by reaction not argument

The question: given your 9 real Favourite Albums (pulled from MusicBrainz with their real tags), does a simple deterministic tag-overlap Engine surface Candidates you'd actually want? Press the toggles below and watch the ranked list change. There's no right answer here — your reaction to what moves and what doesn't is the answer. Design decisions this settles get written up in ticket 012, not here.

"Exclude Favourite artists" only excludes artists from your 9 Favourites (Westside Gunn, etc.) — it does nothing about a Candidate artist appearing more than once within the results (e.g. two Pink Floyd albums, neither a Favourite). "One album per artist in results" is the separate toggle for that.

"Boost genre tags" multiplies a tag's weight 2.5x if it's genre-shaped ("gangsta rap", "art rock") rather than a mood/theme/trivia tag ("existential", "car on cover") — a hand-written keyword guess standing in for a real Tag taxonomy (ticket 014). Genre tags are bolded in the Reason column below; the rest are plain. Other-tags still count, just at 1x.
